# Tell Liora: the 5-minute demo

One woman, two chapters, rising stakes; every step checked against the real rules on 10 Oct, 6:20 AM. Only the
words Gemma writes on calm turns change from run to run; every decision and every urgent line is
fixed.

## Before you go on stage

- [ ] Final release build (no "Native model test" link). **Open it once with Wi-Fi on** (iOS verifies
      a newly installed developer build online), then turn on **airplane mode**. Wait for **AI on**.

## The flow (5:00): two chapters, one phone

## If something goes wrong

- **Voice misheard:** type the same sentence; the rules are the same.
- **No time to switch her status on stage:** pre-set a second phone as the pregnant chapter.
- **AI not on yet:** use the **Danger-sign checklist**; it feeds the same WHO rules with no AI.
- **A go-now reminder appears where you didn't expect it:** you skipped **↺**; tap it and continue.

## Judge questions (one line each)

- **"If the rules decide, why use AI?"** Gemma turns Taglish, typos and voice into the WHO signs the
  rules check; the word list alone misses how people really talk.
- **"What if the AI is wrong?"** It can only add caution: an unsure sign gets WHO's own question,
  skipping counts as serious, and danger turns show fixed WHO words, never AI text.
- **"Is this a medical device?"** No. It helps her decide when to go, cites WHO and DOH word for
  word, and never diagnoses.
- **"Why local?"** Her body, her pregnancy and her moods never leave the phone, and it works at 2 AM
  with no signal and no per-question cost.
